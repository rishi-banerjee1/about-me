Talent Systems

These systems make hiring judgment visible and repeatable

Hiring Command Center (TA CentCom)

Critical searches lose momentum when teams can see status but cannot see the constraint, the owner, or the decision required.

The command center for the TA engine: where time is being lost, who owns the next move, and what needs action.

↗

Helpful for

TA leaders, People leaders, and executives accountable for critical hiring and the health of the TA engine.

What it enables

Replaces status-driven hiring reviews with a management view of TA engine health, bottlenecks, ownership, and action.

Interview Evaluator

Interviewers rarely receive specific feedback on how their questions, evidence, and judgment affect a hiring decision.

Interviewer coaching and framework work connecting role standards, interview evidence, and practical feedback.

↗

Helpful for

Interviewers, hiring managers, and Talent teams building stronger interviewing capability.

What it enables

Turns completed interviews into practical feedback that interviewers can use in the next conversation.

Sourcing Compass

Teams entering unfamiliar talent markets often begin searching before they have mapped where relevant capability may sit.

An AI talent mapper that turns a job title into a full sourcing landscape in under 10 seconds.

↗

Helpful for

Recruiters and hiring leaders entering specialist or unfamiliar talent markets.

What it enables

Moves sourcing strategy upstream, from a Boolean search to a deliberate map of the market.
