Advisory

Discrete advisory support for founders, CHROs, and leadership teams building or transforming talent capability.

I work with leadership teams when talent has become a business constraint, a transformation mandate, or a question that needs senior ownership. The public work describes the kinds of outcomes I help create. The operating detail stays inside the engagement.

Talent Function Build and Transformation

For startups creating a talent function and enterprises modernising fragmented or legacy models. The work can cover leadership alignment, workforce and hiring strategy, operating rhythm, team capability, and the conditions needed for the function to scale with the business.

Hiring and Interview Architecture

For organisations that need more confidence in the quality and consistency of hiring decisions. The focus is on creating an evaluation environment that gives leadership clearer evidence and a durable standard as the organisation grows.

AI-Native TA and HR Transformation

For teams that want technology to improve the quality and speed of talent work without creating new risk or fragmented process. I help identify where systems can support judgment, how the organisation adopts them, and the governance required to make them durable.

Fractional Leadership and Team Coaching

For leaders who need hands-on senior support through an inflection point: a new market, a capability build, a team reset, or a high-stakes hiring mandate. This can include leadership partnership, operating cadence, recruiter capability, and transformation ownership.

Ways to engage

  • Diagnostic or strategy sprint
  • Defined transformation project
  • Fractional leadership mandate
  • Leadership or TA-team advisory
  • Interviewer or recruiter capability programme
