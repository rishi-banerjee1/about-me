## The problem

I built this to help coordinate multiple AI projects: keeping research, documentation, and release steps connected as the work changes.

## What I built

A coordination layer. Four parallel research workflows vet product ideas against cost, effort, and monetization filters. Fleet management audits all repos for stale docs, hardcoded dates, and missing error handling. Release tooling enforces a consistent ship sequence.

## Why it exists

It is less a product and more an operating system for the rest of the products. It gives me one place to keep track of how the projects are moving.
